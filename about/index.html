Our agency

1.Alpha Employment Agency (MOM License No: 21C0788) is a trusted employment and recruitment agency specializing in foreign domestic helpers, conveniently located in Bukit Timah and serving the Singapore area.

We offer a diverse selection of professional and reliable maids from Indonesia, Myanmar, and the Philippines, catering to your unique household needs.

Whether you're seeking fresh or experienced helpers, our carefully screened and well-trained domestic workers provide excellent service to ensure a clean, organized, and harmonious home.

Choose 1.Alpha Employment Agency for dependable solutions and exceptional support in finding the right helper for your family.

How we get to know each helper

A family looking for company for an older parent may have quite different priorities from one planning around school runs. We build your shortlist around the routines you describe and the experience each helper brings.

Where we recruit

A one-to-one interview

Our assessors speak with each helper individually. When you meet someone on your shortlist, you can explore the details that matter to you. If she has cared for an older person, for example, ask what they did together and what help they needed day to day. She can ask about your family, too.

Practical skills and preparation

Our trainers assess basic household skills. Helpers who complete the interview and practical assessment then work on communication and everyday tasks at a training centre before travelling. Training continues in Singapore so they can become familiar with local household routines.

Support after arrival

We follow up after placement. If settling in has been difficult, give our Bukit Timah team a concrete example. It might be a dinner instruction that keeps getting misunderstood or two tasks that need doing at the same time. That gives us a starting point for a conversation with you and your helper.
