Domestic help
for your home.

From our office in Bukit Timah, we help families across Singapore find domestic helpers from Indonesia, Myanmar and the Philippines. We start with your daily routine, whether you need a hand with school mornings, family meals or care for an older parent.

We’ll introduce you to screened helpers, including women coming to Singapore for the first time and those who have worked here before. You can ask about their training and go through the costs with us before you decide. We’re here for you both as you settle in.

What to bring to your first chat

Make a short list of the tasks you’d like help with, the languages spoken at home and when you hope to start. If care is part of the role, describe what the person can do independently and where they need a hand.

What to ask at the interview

Pick a familiar moment from your day. You might ask, “How would you plan dinner around the children’s school run?” Give the helper time to describe her experience and ask you questions too.
